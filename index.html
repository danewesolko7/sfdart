Your FHA servicing performance, in one place.

SF-DART is the self-service tool from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) for viewing your Tier Ranking System II (TRS II) scores, case-level data behind each score, and quarterly performance reports. Report your training towards the annual training credit.

What's inside

TRS II scores servicers each quarter on four elements of delinquent servicing.

  • Foreclosure Prevention

    How often loss mitigation was attempted before a foreclosure was started.

  • Redefaults

    How loans perform in the six months after a modification or partial claim.

  • SFDMS Reporting

    Compliance with Single Family Default Monitoring System reporting, including fatal errors and neglected defaults.

  • Loss Mitigation Engagement

    Successful loss mitigation claims and reported engagement with borrowers.

Scores are calculated monthly, averaged each quarter, then averaged annually over the October 1 – September 30 fiscal year. Tier 1 servicers receive servicing incentives.

TRS II servicer narrative (PDF)

SF-DART is a business intelligence (BI) and integrated analytics platform for use by HUD and mortgagees to support mortgage servicing, claims, and property disposition. SF-DART supports HUD's goal of assisting homeowners in avoiding foreclosure whenever possible while mitigating losses to the Mutual Mortgage Insurance Fund (MMIF).

Data sources that feed Analytics and trends

  • 7 years of FHA mortgage data
  • Detailed mortgage default information
  • Mortgage insurance claims from servicers
  • Home Equity Conversion Mortgage data

How it works

  1. Request access

    Your organization's staff sign up for an SF-DART account.

  2. Get notified

    You receive an email when new quarterly scores are posted.

  3. Review your scores

    See your grade, tier, and each element's score for the quarter.

  4. Drill into cases

    See the case-level details that make up each element score.
